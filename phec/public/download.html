Pre-hospital Emergency Care mobile application for emergency responders. Submit and manage emergency cases on the go.

✨ Features

  • Submit emergency cases with patient information
  • Upload photos and videos of incidents
  • Record vital signs and triage levels
  • Real-time sync with hospital dashboard
  • Modern card-based UI with gradients
  • Offline support for data entry
